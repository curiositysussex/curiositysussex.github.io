About us

Ideas are for everyone.

We are a small science education charity based in Sussex. We create opportunities for people of all ages to meet the people, projects and questions shaping our world.

Since 2016, we have organised events including the Lewes STEM Fair, which most recently welcomed more than 30 exhibitors and 1,500 visitors. We have also brought people together for the Speakers Symposium, in partnership with Lewes Astronomical Society, and Lewes Stargazing Live.
