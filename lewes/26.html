A festival for every kind of mind

Big questions,
brilliant answers.

The Lewes STEM Festival brings together local researchers, makers, businesses and families to celebrate the ideas shaping our world. Whether you love the stars, bugs, code, machines—or simply finding out how things work—there’s a place for you here.

This year, the festival is focused on one lively, free STEM Fair—full of people, ideas and activities to discover together.
